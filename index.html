05 / About

I learn best by making something that can fail.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Jordan University of Science and Technology. I got into game development by building small Unity projects, then kept pushing the same process further: one mechanic, one system, one complete game.

Publishing changed what I pay attention to. A game is not finished when the core mechanic works. It is finished when saving survives an edge case, the UI works on a phone, the build behaves outside the editor, and somebody else can download it without me standing next to them.

Game development is where I want to work. The AI, embedded, robotics and teaching projects are useful because they made me more comfortable debugging systems and explaining technical decisions.
